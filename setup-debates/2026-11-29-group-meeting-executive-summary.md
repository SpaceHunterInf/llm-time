## 1. One-Paragraph Bottom Line

Since the [23 November group meeting](2026-11-22-group-meeting-brief.md), we resolved every queued benchmark question from last week and completed the pre-training diagnostic for our local `8B` open-weight reviewer (`Qwen/Qwen3-8B`). First, removing the public freeze cue and matching initial losses across a 2-session hidden dip vs. lasting reversal proved that **Verify-Before-Quarantine (`Directive-SGEH-VBQ`)** rescues false post-dip abandonment by **`+28.64 pts`** (`59.0% → 87.7%`) and ranks **`#1` (`85.6%` mean)** across all five 3-window `AlphaDesk` streams. Second, isolating **reviewer self-improvement** (`H = 60` sessions, actor-visible `task_note` + hidden reviewer-only `review_rule`) and auditing checkpoints with **`K = 8` version-matched, strategy-anonymized replays** proved that self-written meta-rules causally move downstream 4-session net returns in **both directions**—producing genuine strategy-free falsification upgrades (**`+27.41 pts, p = 0.0068`** on `s704`; **`+21.12 pts, p = 0.0003`** on `s709`) alongside rule-erasure regressions (**`-19.95 pts, p = 0.0078`** on `s711`), explaining why untuned `Flash` nets **`~0 pts`** live (`-1.23` and `-2.05 pts` vs. `Frozen-Reviewer`) while `Hydrogen` (`#1`, `84.0%` `W4`) writes strategy-agnostic rules out of the box. Finally, testing untuned **`Qwen/Qwen3-8B`** on `8` fresh streams (`M = 32` preselected Keep-or-Apply decisions, **`CoalitionOps` 100% untouched** for held-out transfer) caught two critical pre-training bottlenecks: **(a)** a **`93.3%` rubber-stamp `APPLY` bias** (`matching Always-Apply to 0.00 pts on all 8/8 seeds` and accepting `3/3` harmful edits up to `-6.79 pts`), and **(b)** a **proposal-quality ceiling (`Oracle − Keep = +0.17 pts`, `0/32` proposals `>= +2.0 pts`)**, proving `Act 3` LoRA RL must jointly train **both candidate rule proposal and `KEEP/APPLY` gatekeeping**.

## 3. Candid Audit of Negative Results & Methodological Catches

1. **Single-Rollout Replay False Positive (`Flash s705`: `+28.73%` $\to$ `-22.36 pts` at `K = 8`, `p = 0.0078`):** Initial single-draw replay favored the revised rule, but `K = 8` version-matched trials showed `R_ckpt` accidentally benefited from naming `W1` winner `S4` when `S4` recurred in `A'` (stripping `S4` dropped `Initial` by `-19.35 pts`), while `R_pre_W4`'s `< 45` trigger missed `A'`'s `88 → 47` drop. **Lesson:** Multi-trial (`K >= 6`) strategy-anonymized rollouts are mandatory for training/evaluation labels.
2. **Second-Order Rule-Erasure Ratchet (`Flash s711`: `-19.95 pts` anonymized, `p = 0.0078`):** `s711` *already* had a working `< 45` falsification rule at session 12 (`70.2%` DP across `K = 8`), then overwrote it with *"Keep probes disabled unless desk performance collapses"* (`50.3%` across `8/8` trials, `0` probes).
3. **Joint-Call Copy Inertia vs. Separated Proposal Sycophancy on `Qwen/Qwen3-8B`:** In our Pair-1 diagnostic (`gate8b_s721–722`), asking greedy `Qwen3-8B` to update `task_note` and `review_rule` in one JSON call resulted in `7/8` (`87.5%`) verbatim copies of `current_rule`. Separating proposal (`T = 0.6`) from `KEEP/APPLY` gatekeeping (`T = 0.0`) yielded `30/32` distinct proposals—where `Qwen3-8B` then rubber-stamped `93.3%` of its own rewrites and never once proposed a `< X → probe` falsification rule worth `>= +2.0 pts`.
4. **Sparse Binary-Feedback Ceiling on Dev `ServeLearnBench` (`slb_banking_l3_evenstride_6win`):** Fixing public `TXN-/LIM-/TRF-` scope routing raised `General` doc compliance to `93.3%` (`45.0%` overall vs. `42.5%` `Blind` at half the reflection calls), but `< 12` binary `0/1` cases/window across `15–28` rule families underdetermines multi-clause `L3` shifts—requiring full-stream evaluation (`50–100` cases/window).
