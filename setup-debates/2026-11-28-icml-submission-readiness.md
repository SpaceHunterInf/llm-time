# Could the agent-led research support an ICML 2027 paper?

## Codex assessment · 28 November 2026

I would aim for an ICML main-track submission. I would lean against acceptance on the present evidence alone. The strongest paper is an evaluation of agents that rewrite their own learning instructions as rewards change. ICML’s **Datasets, Evaluations and Benchmarks** contribution type explicitly welcomes advances in evaluation methodology. The abstract is due **14 January 2027** and the full paper **22 January 2027**, both at 11:59 p.m. Anywhere on Earth. [Contribution types](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/ContributionTypes) · [Call for papers](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers)

**The claim we can support.** An agent writes advice for its future actions and a separate rule for how to revise that advice. We can hold the actor, public observations and incoming advice fixed, swap the reviewer’s rule, and measure what the actor subsequently earns after fees. In one AlphaDesk case, a single replay suggested a 28.73-point improvement. Across eight trials per rule with the displayed version held fixed, the revised rule averaged 22.36 points worse. On a fresh ending, two selected cases showed opposite effects even after removing strategy names from both rules, +21.12 and −19.95 points. Across all six fresh lifetimes, the editable reviewer scored 64.62% of optimal return in the final phase, against 66.67% with the reviewer frozen. The experiment establishes that particular self-written rules can help or hurt. It leaves the typical benefit and cross-task generality uncertain. [Original replay](../results/runs/meta_reviewer_h60_flash_n6/summary.md) · [Repeated trials](../results/runs/repeated_rule_replay_stage1_k8/summary.md) · [All six fresh lifetimes](../results/runs/meta_dip_c_h60_flash_n6/summary.md)

**What a reviewer could reasonably challenge.** Other groups have already shown that frozen agents can write procedures and that self-modifying agents can overwrite useful knowledge on drifting tasks. Our strongest contribution is the controlled measurement of what a self-written review rule changes downstream. The repeated trials sample model responses at selected AlphaDesk checkpoints. The banking pilot used a transferred, frozen procedure and answers a different question. [Self-authored procedures in games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23219) · [Self-modifying agents on drifting tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25858) · [Banking pilot](../results/runs/slb_banking_l3_evenstride_6win/summary.md)

**The next piece of evidence I would prioritize.** Freeze the comparison before inspecting outcomes in a second task, such as banking cases with changing service policies. At preselected review times, compare the current rule and its proposed replacement using the same observed history, actor and tool budget. Look at subsequent decisions and utility alongside always keeping or always accepting edits. Report variation across independent streams. A reproducible benefit, harm, or well-estimated limit would each inform the paper. This also provides an evaluation for a future 8B reviewer. Weight training can proceed on its own timeline.

**Human review and disclosure.** I would start a draft now. By the project’s mid-December draft milestone, have a human coauthor trace one win and one reversal from the saved public inputs to the actions and scores, and reproduce the headline table. Mark where checkpoints were selected after seeing results and verify the closest citations. Record what Gemini and Codex designed, implemented, ran and interpreted, what the PI directed, and what the human authors verified afterward. ICML requires an AI Disclosure Statement and holds human authors responsible for the whole paper. AI used as an experimental component also belongs in the methods.
