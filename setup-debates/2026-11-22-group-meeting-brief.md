## Opening for a group new to the pivot

Imagine an agent repeatedly choosing between six strategies. Yesterday’s best strategy begins paying badly. It has a reason to reconsider. Now imagine its usual strategy still pays as before, but an option it rarely uses has quietly improved. What would make it pay to look? The pivot is to study those decisions within one continuing lifetime, where remembering past successes can help or hinder adaptation.

**What we built.** AlphaDesk is a controlled simulator. Version 0.3 carries holdings across sessions, charges for switching, and allows a single paid observation of a strategy’s current noisy yield. In the six-window benchmark, payoffs can decay, reverse or partially return. Our external check uses changing customer-service policy on ServeLearnBench. Its cases and scoring rules come from another project. LifeCompanion and CoalitionOps are additional in-house prototypes. CEO-Bench was an analogy for eventual depth. ReScopeBench is a design reference. Neither supplies a result in this briefing. [AlphaDesk implementation](../src/envs/alpha_desk_v3.py) · [Environment-depth discussion](2026-11-19-environment-depth.md)

**What we used.** The controlled comparison used frozen Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Gemma 4 31B IT through Google’s API. A harness is the software providing cross-session memory and instructions. Rule cards stored ranked advice. A factual ledger recorded observed actions, outcomes and the age of evidence. The directed arm added human-written instructions to the same ledger. The banking study used Gemini 3.8 Flash. DeepSeek was discussed as a candidate. The reported comparisons contain no DeepSeek run or model training. [Controlled comparison](../results/runs/autonomy_cross_model_n6/summary.md) · [Banking comparison](../results/runs/slb_banking_l3_6win/summary.md)

## Where the guidance came from

**AlphaDesk.** The access audit found public observations on the acting path. At test time the directed harness can tell the agent to hold during a publicly flagged freeze and to check the oldest lightly held strategy. The rule uses a holding below 15%, at least seven calm sessions since a check and at least four since the last scheduled check. If the observed yield beats the expected portfolio mean by more than eight points, it prescribes a 55/45 allocation. A paid observation reveals a noisy current yield. In Flash seed 505 the first check of S1 returned 82.1 in the improving world and 18.1 in the stable twin. The evaluator knows the payoff schedule. The experimenter used private strategy roles to script a shared starting history. This weakens any claim about discovering that history or inventing the checking rule unaided. [Controller instructions](../src/harnesses/sgeh_v3.py) · [Shared-history construction](../scripts/run_cross_model_autonomy_fork.py) · [Improving trace](../results/raw/autonomy_s505_flash/alpha_desk_v3_fork_opportunity_rise__sgeh_directive_v3__gemini-3.8-flash_s505.jsonl) · [Stable trace](../results/raw/autonomy_s505_flash/alpha_desk_v3_fork_opportunity_stationary__sgeh_directive_v3__gemini-3.8-flash_s505.jsonl)

**ServeLearnBench.** The model wrote advice from public outcomes on previous serving cases. We supplied workflow keywords, the review threshold and the instruction to revisit archived rules. Test memory was frozen for each window. The separate oracle received the active policy, and the runner used private labels to balance the development slice. The code audit found no path from a held-out verdict or oracle policy note to our acting harness. The traces lack the full text of every generated rule. The AlphaDesk and banking controllers implement related ideas with different code and instructions. An earlier general banking variant scored 5/14 against 9/14 for the memoryless agent on a different stream. ## Feasibility of the other setups and training

AlphaDesk can generate paired trajectories under controlled changes. ServeLearnBench already supplies banking cases, public serving feedback and held-out scoring. The integration lets us ask whether an observation from a simulator appears in a task designed elsewhere. The existing LifeCompanion and CoalitionOps prototypes can exercise the interface, but the earlier three-seed results were uneven. On CoalitionOps the proposed harness and strongest baseline were within half a point. A convincing held-out setup still needs meaningful action consequences, checks with real costs, and cases where the best decision varies. [Cross-setup review](2026-11-19-pi-charter-review.md#codex-review-of-iteration-2-and-external-results--2026-11-20)

The 144 exported training examples inspected so far repeat the same incumbent-loss cue. They offer little supervision for distinguishing a temporary shock from a lasting change. The conference contribution we can defend today is the controlled separation of memory representation and human guidance, the measured cost of exploration, and a limited external comparison. A stronger claim about learning a portable checking procedure depends on the next experiments. [Training-data review](2026-11-21-alphadesk-v03-multishift-spec.md#codex-interpretation-and-next-experiment--2026-11-21)

## The next decision

1. **Queued benchmark refinement.** Give a temporary dip and a lasting reversal the same initial loss and free public signals. Offer a paid diagnostic that distinguishes them. In the existing test, the market status explicitly identified a liquidity freeze, and the evidence-only Gemini models already achieved 99.9% and 100% there. Advance one environment change at a time. [Current queue](../RESEARCH_STATE.md#4-next-queued-milestone-awaiting-explicit-pi-go--low-priority-backlog)
2. **Proposed test of an agent-authored procedure.** Give a frozen agent the objective, legal tools, costs, public feedback and an initially blank procedure. At a common checkpoint, let subsequent edits take effect in one branch and keep the checkpoint procedure frozen in another. Both branches receive new observations and equal review opportunities. Save the text before future outcomes arrive, compare later actions and net rewards, and include a stable stream. A Bayesian confidence estimate or value-of-information rule is a candidate the agent might discover. Its value has to appear in subsequent decisions. [Procedure proposal](2026-11-19-learning-to-revise-heuristics.md#codex-reply-on-an-agent-revising-its-own-harness--2026-11-22)
3. **Transfer and recursion.** Freeze the generic procedure before selecting a held-out setup and start that setup with empty task memory. Measure whether an edit improves later revisions as well as immediate actions. The current results support the benchmark and a comparison of harnesses. Discovering a transferable heuristic and demonstrating recursive self-improvement remain open. Training stays deferred while the benchmark, rollouts and supervision are under development. [Transfer criterion and novelty discussion](2026-11-19-learning-to-revise-heuristics.md)
