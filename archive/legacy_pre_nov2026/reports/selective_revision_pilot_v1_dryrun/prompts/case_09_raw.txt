You are given a fully stated generative model and public evidence. Infer the requested probabilities and choose one action. Do not assume an event occurred unless the stated model says the hazard is 1.

Components A, B, and C each have a binary latent reward rate: low 0.2 or high 0.8. Independently of the others, each starts high with probability 0.99. That prior already summarizes earlier evidence; you are not given raw prehistory.

There is at most one possible reset before the observation batch. No component changes during the batch.

A and B have independent reset events. Each occurs with probability 0.1 and uses the same redraw rule: a reset redraws that component's rate as high or low with probability 0.5. C cannot reset in this interval.

You will see evidence about A only. There is no fresh evidence about B or C.

Choose exactly one action:
- choose_B: take B's unknown expected reward, equal to 0.2 + 0.6 * P(B is high).
- choose_safe: take a known alternative worth 0.65.
- diagnose_B: pay 0.04 once to learn B's latent RATE (not a future reward), then take the higher of that rate and 0.65. The cost is charged exactly once even if the safe option is chosen afterward. Choosing diagnose_B commits to using the revealed rate optimally; this probe tests valuation of information, not interpretation of diagnostic results.

Evidence on A after the possible reset, in order (1 = success, 0 = failure): [0, 0, 0, 0, 0]. B and C were not observed.

Return exactly one JSON object and no other text:
{
  "probability_high": {
    "A": <posterior probability that A's latent rate is high>,
    "B": <posterior probability that B's latent rate is high>,
    "C": <posterior probability that C's latent rate is high>
  },
  "predictive_mean": {
    "A": <expected latent reward rate of A>,
    "B": <expected latent reward rate of B>,
    "C": <expected latent reward rate of C>
  },
  "action": "choose_B" or "choose_safe" or "diagnose_B"
}

probability_high.X is P(the latent rate of X equals the high rate | permitted evidence and the stated model). It is not a verbal confidence rating.
predictive_mean.X is the expected latent rate of X, equal to low + (high-low) * probability_high.X, and is also the expected Bernoulli reward if that rate is used.
action is the unique choice among the three options above.
Do not include a rationale field.
