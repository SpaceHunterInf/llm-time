## Direction

Study **whether acquired improvement procedures produce transferable gains over sustained use after accounting for their cost**. Freeze model weights; persist executable solver and improver code plus a separately tracked experience archive. Start with stationary CPU resource-allocation instances, then test hidden change only if persistent improvement works. The host owns immutable evaluation, budgets and deployment. A later transfer experiment independently resets or preserves solver and improver to distinguish better solutions from a better improvement procedure.

Method and novelty status: no established new-method contribution. Implement a two-level propose–test–retain baseline, then identify a reproducible limitation before proposing a new intervention. STOP, PILOT, ModularRSI, Dream-RSI and RRSI directly overlap generic self-editing, persistent skills, exploration improvement, modular edits and regularized acceptance. The [25 September literature checkpoint](research-long-trajectory-self-improvement-commander.md#literature-checkpoint-25-september-2026) records these overlaps. Long trajectories, cost awareness, selective edits and OOD evaluation are not sufficient novelty claims. Compare with fixed improvement and ordinary search at matched total resources; later test transfer of the improver from a common starting solver. The paired loss remains retired.

The earlier eight-week proposal is too large as an initial commitment. Gemini diagnostics, the ordinary-KL learning pilot and the bounded numeric optimization diagnosis are complete. About 78% of LoRA's structural KL gain over head-only comes from h=0 cases, where reset grouping is dynamically inactive. The independent filter's structural decision regret is only 0.000753, versus 0.008568 for LoRA; the task has little average decision headroom over this cheap reference. Task 010 shows that the numeric learner benefits from longer training, without establishing LLM necessity or novelty. Do not spend more compute to avoid making the research-value decision. Defer RL, larger sweeps and workflow integration. The worker records user authorization for the completed nine-run local GPU pilot; do not infer blanket authorization for future GPU training. ## Literature checkpoint

Two primary sources were rechecked on 9 September; this is not a renewed exhaustive novelty review. Shared versus independent change structure already has a cognitive precedent in [Heilbron and Meyniel (2019)](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1006972), which uses transfer of uncertainty between coupled statistics to distinguish inference models. [MemoPilot](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08656) already trains a memory updater for a frozen player using multi-turn reinforcement learning. Therefore neither the diagnostic motif nor generic learned memory supplies novelty alone. The research bet remains an improvement in selective decisions and structural transfer under matched information and compute; the pilot may reject it.
